Wireless EEG Headset

More about Anthriq

Anthriq’s Instinct is a non-invasive wireless EEG headset with 21-channels.
This case study presents selected highlights from my graduation project.
A detailed project report covering research, mechanism development, prototyping, testing, and design decisions is available on request.

Sponsor

Client

Formerly Nexstem

Context

Design a non-invasive, wireless EEG headset for researchers and developers, intended for clinical environments with cerebral palsy patients as key subjects.
The headset needed to support dry electrodes, universal fit, comfort, stable electrode contact, wireless use, and a product language that felt safe, inviting, and medically appropriate.

Key design features

Universal DesignHeadset fits various head shapes and sizes

Onboard ProcessingProcesses signals on headset

High Resolution19 channels to acquire data

Automated ContactMotorised Electrode movement
