Glaviqem Lab Oven.

A scalable hot air oven platform for research and industrial laboratory environments.

Context & researchConceptsDesign directionScaleDetails

A platform for the laboratory.

Commissioned by Glaviqem, the project focused on designing a robust oven platform that could adapt across multiple chamber volumes while keeping manufacturing simple, durable and production-friendly.

Context

The design had to work around fixed heating chamber and electronics constraints, while creating a clean exterior system that could scale across different oven sizes.

The body uses sheet-metal panels with simple cutting, bending and powder-coating processes, suitable for the expected production scale and the durability needs of lab environments.

Research

The project began with research into institutional and company labs, technician workflows, equipment usage and existing lab oven designs. This identified opportunities around usability, durability, service access, visual trust and manufacturing simplicity.

Three directions. One platform.

Atlas, Prime and Edge explore clean geometric structure and softer, more approachable surfaces.

The concepts focused on overall form direction. Once the final direction was selected, the details were refined further for manufacturing and prototyping.

Precision with approachability.

The selected direction combines geometric strength with subtle curved surfaces.

This helped the oven feel robust and professional, while avoiding the harsh and purely box-like appearance common in lab equipment.
