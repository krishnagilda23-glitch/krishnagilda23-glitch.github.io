Design process

The design direction was inspired by Japanese mecha and industrial machinery, with the goal of making the robot feel strong, serviceable, and factory-ready.

Key design decisionsdecision

Exposed carbon-fibre tubes

The exposed carbon-fibre tubes highlight the robot’s strength and material quality.

Visible fastening points

Screw holes and fasteners were kept visible in selected areas to give the product a more industrial, serviceable, and robust character.

Modular body structure

The robot was visually and structurally divided into key modules — shoulder, arm, and body sections. This helped keep component sizes manageable and supported easier assembly, disassembly, and troubleshooting.

Integrated top and base design

The upper humanoid structure and the mobile base were designed to feel like one connected system, not two separate products joined together.
