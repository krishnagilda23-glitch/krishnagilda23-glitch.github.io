The Chai Maker.

A concept for making chai at home, with control over flavour and the familiar brewing process.

Ingredients & controlsMilk & waterBrewing & straining

Your chai, your way.

The front section stores the ingredients, while the control panel lets users customise the flavour.

Visible ingredient quantities communicate both what is available and how the machine prepares chai.

A place for milk and water.

Two removable, transparent containers at the back make their contents easy to see.

Brew and strain in one pot.

Opening the lid gives access to the heating pot and its attached strainer.

The fine metal mesh strainer latches inside the heating pot, keeping strained chai at the bottom and removing the need for a separate straining step.
