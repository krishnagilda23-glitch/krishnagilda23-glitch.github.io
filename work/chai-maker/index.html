The Chai Maker

The Automated Chai Maker is a concept product designed to replicate the authentic process of making chai at home, ensuring you never have to compromise on taste just because it’s prepared by a machine. It offers precise control over flavors, allowing you to tailor your chai to perfectly suit your taste buds. ☕✨

The front section stores all the ingredients except for water and milk

The Control Panel allows users easy access to customise the flavor of their Chai

It serves two purposes
1. Visually displaying the material quantity, and 2. Reinforcing the product’s capability to make quality Chai

Back section has a lid, covering the heating pot.There are two removable transparent containers below for milk and water,showing their quantity

Opening the lid allows access to heating pot and attached strainer

The fine metal mesh strainer latches securely inside the heating pot,keeping the strained Chai at the bottom.This design eliminates the need for a separate straining process.
