Ati Edubot

More about Ati Robotics

Edubot is a compact autonomous mobile robot platform built for academic use. It gives students and researchers a hands-on way to learn robotics, test ideas, and experiment with both software and hardware.

My role

Industrial design, research, moodboarding, concept development, CAD, exterior panel design, internal architecture planning, design detailing, engineering coordination, and DFM support.

A Peg board like pattern around the robot

A grid of holes lets students add sensors, brackets, and custom 3D-printed parts for quick testing and experimentation.

Bent-Tube Chassis for Heavy Components

Larger components can be mounted directly onto the bent-tube chassis, giving students a stronger base for testing heavier hardware.

Structure as Protection

The exposed chassis doubles as a protective bumper.

I/O Ports

Side I/O ports let students connect different hardware modules easily.

Utility Space

A small open area gives students space to keep tools, connectors, and small parts while working around the robot.

Battery Panel

The exposed chassis also acts as a protective bumper, helping safeguard parts

Single panel access to internal components

Opening one panel gives quick access to the internal electronics, making troubleshooting, demonstration, and learning easier.

Standardised mounting channels

Built-in mounting channels allow students to attach cobots, sensors, and different payloads for testing.

LED Visual Interface

The front LED matrix helps the robot communicate status, alerts, and feedback clearly to students.
