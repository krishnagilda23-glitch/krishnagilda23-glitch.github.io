Ati Sherpa XT Lite

More about Ati Robotics

An indoor–outdoor autonomous tugger designed for trolley movement in factories and warehouses.
Sherpa XT Lite is built to move heavy trolleys across real industrial environments, while working around people, equipment, and existing shop-floor layouts.

My role

Industrial design, exterior form development, CAD refinement, design detailing, visual language development, and DFM support in collaboration with engineering teams.

Brief

Sherpa XT Lite was designed before I joined Ati Robotics.
My brief was to refine the existing design language while preserving its original identity. The goal was to make the robot feel cleaner, tougher, and more world-class, without making it look like a completely new product.
