Ati Omni Wheel.

More about Ati Robotics

A motorised caster wheel for controlled AMR movement in multiple directions.

Context & briefDesign decisionsGallery

A module with its own identity.

The project focused on turning a functional wheel module into a robust industrial product with its own identity: strong enough for AMR use, and refined enough to stand as an independent product.

Context

The wheel was developed as a drive and steering module for AMRs. The challenge was to design around fixed motor and mechanical constraints while creating a compact, serviceable and industrial-looking enclosure.

Brief

Design a motorised caster wheel that feels strong, rugged and production-ready. The form had to work within tight internal constraints, support serviceability and carry a distinct product identity beyond its use inside an AMR.

Built around the mechanism.

Robust cast body

The metal body was shaped to feel solid, protective and suitable for industrial use.

Serviceable architecture

The enclosure was detailed to support easier access, maintenance and part replacement.

Industrial detailing

Strong surfaces and graphic detailing helped give the wheel a rugged, high-quality character.
