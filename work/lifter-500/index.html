Ati Lifter 500

More about Ati Robotics

Lifter 500 is a compact lifting Autonomous Mobile Robot (AMR) designed for flexible material movement in factories and warehouses. It can handle loads up to 500 kg while maintaining a compact footprint.

Brief

Design a compact lifting AMR with a strong visual identity.
Using only simple sheet metal bending operations,
Fixed LED and HMI dimensions.

Key design decisions

Modular Panels, Fewer Variants

Modular panels improved access and troubleshooting, while identical front-rear and left-right panels reduced part variants and simplified manufacturing.

Accessible Emergency Stop

The emergency switch was placed at an angle for easy access and can also be operated by foot when needed.

Speaker as an Identity

The speaker grille was integrated into the front design, making it part of the AMR’s visual identity.
