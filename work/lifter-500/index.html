Ati Lifter 500.

More about Ati Robotics

A compact lifting AMR for material movement in factories and warehouses.

My roleDesign briefDesign decisionsGallery

Compact form. Industrial purpose.

Lifter 500 handles loads up to 500 kg while maintaining a compact footprint for flexible material movement.

My role

  • Industrial design
  • Research
  • Concept development
  • CAD
  • Detailing
  • DFM and execution support

Working within the constraints.

Create a compact lifting robot with a strong visual identity and a straightforward manufacturing approach.

  • Simple sheet-metal bending operations
  • Fixed LED dimensions
  • Fixed HMI dimensions

Fewer parts. Clearer identity.

Panel commonality, accessible controls and an integrated speaker grille shaped the design.

Modular panels, fewer variants

Modular panels support access and troubleshooting. Identical front–rear and left–right panels reduce part variants and simplify manufacturing.

Accessible emergency stop

An angled emergency switch makes it easier to reach and allows foot operation when needed.

The speaker as an identity

The speaker grille is integrated into the front panel, becoming part of the robot’s visual identity.
